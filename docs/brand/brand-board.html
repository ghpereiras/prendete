Prendete · Brand guide

Prendete is how an Argentine invites you to a plan (“¿te prendés?”), and prender also means to switch on. So the p of the name lights up. The wordmark says it, and the lit p alone is the app icon: one idea, recognizable at any size.

Logo

Violet on light is the primary version. White on violet or on dark. Ink only where color isn't possible.

App icon

The last one is the Android adaptive version, cropped to a circle.

One ink

For stamps, embroidery or single-color print: the p's counter stays open.

Typeface

Wordmark: Fredoka SemiBold (SIL Open Font License), tracking −0.6%, the font's own kerning. Outlined in the SVGs, so no font install is needed.

Shortlisted against Bricolage Grotesque (more character, but its narrow counter makes the light read as a squeezed oval) and Outfit (cleanest, but generic). Fredoka is the one that sounds like a get-together: round, warm, informal without being childish.

Voice

¿Te prendés?
The name already is the call to action. Use it as the headline for invites and campaigns.

Usage rules

  • The light lives only inside the p. Don't add amber anywhere else in the logo.
  • Clear space: at least the height of the p's counter on every side.
  • Minimum size: the wordmark at 80 px wide; below that use the app icon.
  • Don't stretch, outline, rotate, add shadows, or change the letter spacing.
  • Don't set the wordmark in another typeface or retype it: use the SVG files.
